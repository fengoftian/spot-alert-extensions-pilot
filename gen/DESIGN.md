# Independent B0–B3 prospective comparison

Owner request, 9 October 2026: create a second public repository for B0–B3 while A0–A3 finishes unchanged, using free GitHub Actions. Public data and simulated screening only. No exchange accounts, keys or orders. The A study, its dates and all frozen identities are preserved.

## Question and registered comparisons

Do earlier coin-relative acceleration, BTC-relative strength, buyer-initiated turnover or retest entry timing improve after-cost continuous whole-account screening outcomes over a contemporaneous A2 control?

Five alternative models: C_A2, B0, B1, B2 and B3. Four descriptive contrasts: B0−C_A2, B1−C_A2, B2−C_A2 and B3−C_A2. No combination search, parameter search, best-strategy promotion or interim economics. The numerical design choices below are frozen before prospective observation; they are not optimality claims.

## Exact alerts

Completed UTC one-minute candles only. Unknown features are not false; missing baselines suppress rising edges until false has subsequently been observed. No artificial initial event for conditions already true during warmup. One attempted event per symbol/model per rolling 24 hours, including rejected entries. BTC/USDT is in the cohort: its own BTC-relative ratio is constant and therefore B1 never qualifies it; no hedging or shorting occurs.

- **C_A2:** exactly the A study's A2 feature construction: trailing 60-minute close return ≥10%; hourly USDT turnover ≥3× median turnover of the corresponding trailing hour on the preceding seven UTC days; close strictly above the highest high of the preceding non-overlapping 24 hours, excluding the signal hour. The underlying seven-day continuity and positive-baseline requirements are unchanged. It is a new future-window control, not reuse of old outcomes.
- **B0:** trailing 15-minute close return ≥max(1%, coin's lagged 99th-percentile return); turnover ≥3× median turnover in the corresponding trailing 15-minute intervals on the preceding seven UTC days; close above the preceding non-overlapping 24-hour high, excluding the signal 15 minutes. Calibration uses 2,880 consecutive, non-overlapping UTC 15-minute close returns from the previous 30 complete UTC days, plus the preceding closing observation as anchor. Quantile is nearest rank, ceil(0.99×2880). The baseline ends at the start of the UTC day containing the signal candle's OPEN time, so a candle closing at midnight cannot enter its own baseline. Refresh once per UTC day using a fixed algorithm, never optimization. Complete calibration and seven-day feature continuity are required. B0 changes a detector bundle; differences cannot identify each component's individual effect.
- **B1:** C_A2 plus the synchronized coin/USDT close divided by BTC/USDT close strictly above every minute-close ratio in that same preceding non-overlapping 24-hour reference window. Require every matched BTC observation. This is a ratio feature, not a beta-neutral portfolio.
- **B2:** C_A2 plus sum(taker-buy USDT turnover)/sum(total USDT turnover) ≥60% over the latest five completed minutes. Reject missing/malformed inputs or a zero denominator. This measures trade initiation, not informed demand, participant count or full order-flow imbalance.
- **B3:** start a retest watch only on a rising C_A2 event, and freeze that event's prior-range high H. Within the next 15 minutes, find the first consecutive completed candle pair where first.low ≤H≤first.close and second.close >first.high. The second candle must close no later than signal+15 minutes. Wait for both closes; no use of forming candles or future observations. If no valid pair exists by expiry, reject. Confirmation shifts planned entry time; it is a selection-plus-timing experiment.

## Common execution and risk

Same unchanged common execution engine bytes as the A study, separately pinned here. C_A2/B0/B1/B2: planned public-book observation at signal close+60 seconds, with a 120-second grace. B3 uses retest-confirmation close+60 seconds with the same grace. Late recovered patterns never create past fills. Request duration ≤5 seconds; metadata/lot/minimum-notional controls apply. A 50% displayed-depth haircut and maximum measured book cost 50 bps per side are entry gates. A public book is hypothetical availability, not an actual fill.

45 separate continuous simulated accounts: five models × capital points 250/2000/2500 × 15/50/300 bps shortfall per side. These are alternative accounts, not a combined allocation. The owner's 2000-USDT evaluation is primary; the other points test scale. No real capital is allocated.

Fee assumption 0.10% per side. Nominal risk fraction 0.25% of starting capital: 0.625/5/6.25 USDT at 250/2000/2500; not a guaranteed loss cap. Same maximum clip = risk/(6% stop+0.5% baseline cost buffer), about 3.846% of starting capital. Round quantity down using the 300-bps scenario and fee ceiling, common across scenarios. No compounding-up, averaging down, leverage, margin, derivatives or borrowing. Two positions maximum. Same close-based 6% price stop and four-hour holding expiry measured from actual hypothetical entry; no take profit or tuned exit. Exit uses the next available valid book and records delay/unresolved inventory. Measured observed book costs and scenario shortfalls are combined by max(), not double-counted.

Permanent 3% continuous account breaker, initially 7.50/60/75 USDT at the three capitals, triggers liquidation attempts and entry halt; gaps can overshoot. This remains inside the binding ≤15% objective ceiling, initially 300 USDT at 2000. Maximum 24 entries per model/account in 30 days; fee allowance 3%×30/365 of starting capital. Fee/entry gates never prevent risk-reducing exits. Controls will not be loosened to rescue outcomes.

## Interpretation and stopping

No interim outcome ranking. Report all four contrasts, rejected/missed events and coverage, sample size by symbol/UTC-day cluster, continuous entire-account period return and drawdown, deployment including idle cash, fees and shortfalls, BTC/cash references, capital filters and unresolved/stale inventory. At least 20 accepted union clusters is a descriptive screening convention, not a power calculation. Variants and overlapping windows are correlated; there is no qualified formal inference or independent replication from mere overlap. Costs, missed opportunities and fixed limits remain part of the outcome. Incomplete runtime/data is explicitly incomplete, never a zero-return finding. No CAGR for 30 days, no full-cycle qualification, no “best” strategy or live authority. Stop at the fixed tail and deliver the final screen.
